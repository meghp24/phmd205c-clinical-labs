## Front

Delta gap: HCO3 fell MORE than the AG rose. Concurrent disorder?

## Back

Non-gap (normal AG) metabolic acidosis

## Extra

The slide text reads "non-gap alkalosis"; the standard interpretation is a concurrent non-gap acidosis, since extra HCO3 loss is acid. Confirm with your instructor.
