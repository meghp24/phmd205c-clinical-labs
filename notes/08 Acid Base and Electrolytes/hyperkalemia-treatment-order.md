## Text

Hyperkalemia with ECG changes, in order: {{c1::stabilize the myocardium}} → {{c2::shift K into cells}} → {{c3::eliminate K}}

## Extra

Calcium gluconate stabilizes; insulin with dextrose and albuterol shift; loop diuretic, binder or dialysis eliminate.

Source: PHMD 205C, Acid Base and Electrolyte Disorders.
