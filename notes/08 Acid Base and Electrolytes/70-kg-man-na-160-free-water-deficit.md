## Front

70 kg man, Na 160. Free water deficit?

## Back

About 6 L

## Extra

TBW = 0.6 × weight (men) or 0.5 × weight (women).

Water deficit = TBW × [(Na / 140) − 1] = (0.6 × 70) × (160/140 − 1) = 42 × 0.143 ≈ 6 L.

Source: PHMD 205C, Acid Base and Electrolyte Disorders.
