## Text

Salicylate dialysis threshold: > 100 mg/dL, or > {{c1::90}} mg/dL with kidney impairment.

## Extra

Dialyze regardless of level for altered mental status, new hypoxemia or failure of standard therapy.

Source: PHMD 205C, Toxicology.
