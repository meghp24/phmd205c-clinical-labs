## Front

Hemolysis: effect on measured serum potassium?

## Back

Falsely elevated

## Extra

RBCs spill intracellular K. Median K rose from about 3.8 to 6.8 mEq/L across hemolysis grades 0 to 4. Mg, iron and phosphorus are also falsely raised.

Source: PHMD 205C, Clinical Laboratory Techniques and Analysis.
