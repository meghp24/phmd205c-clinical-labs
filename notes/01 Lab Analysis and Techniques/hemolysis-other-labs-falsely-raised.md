## Front

Besides potassium, which labs does hemolysis falsely raise?

## Back

Mg, phosphorus, iron, LDH and AST

## Extra

All are concentrated inside RBCs and leak into the sample when cells lyse.

Source: PHMD 205C, Clinical Laboratory Techniques and Analysis.
