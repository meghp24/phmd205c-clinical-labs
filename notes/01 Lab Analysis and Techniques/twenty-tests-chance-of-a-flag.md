## Front

A healthy person has 20 independent tests, each with a 95% reference interval. Chance at least one is flagged?

## Back

About 64% (1 − 0.95²⁰)

## Extra

Each interval leaves out 1 in 20 healthy people, so a mildly abnormal result in a well patient can be normal variation.

Source: PHMD 205C, Clinical Laboratory Techniques and Analysis.
