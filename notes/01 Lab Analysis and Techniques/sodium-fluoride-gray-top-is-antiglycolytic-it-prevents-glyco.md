## Text

Sodium fluoride (gray top) is {{c1::antiglycolytic}}: it prevents glycolysis in the sample for {{c2::3 days}}.

## Extra

Useful for glucose samples, since cells keep consuming glucose in the tube.

Source: PHMD 205C, Clinical Laboratory Techniques and Analysis.
