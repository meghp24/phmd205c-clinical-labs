## Front

Severe CAP patient on empiric therapy has a POSITIVE MRSA nares swab. Does this alone justify adding vancomycin?

## Back

No. The swab's strength is ruling out MRSA when negative; a positive only shows colonization

## Extra

Base the decision on cultures and MRSA risk factors.
