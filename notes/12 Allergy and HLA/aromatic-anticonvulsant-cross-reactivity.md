## Front

DRESS from phenytoin: which other anticonvulsants should be avoided?

## Back

Aromatic ones: carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine

## Extra

Choose a non aromatic agent such as levetiracetam. A negative patch test does not make re-exposure safe.

Source: PHMD 205C, Allergy and Human Leukocyte Antigen.
