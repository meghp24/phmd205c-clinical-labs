## Text

Procalcitonin half life: about {{c1::24}} hours.

## Extra

So once infection is controlled, PCT should fall by about half each day. A PCT that won't fall points to uncontrolled infection.

Source: PHMD 205C, Biomarkers of Inflammation and Infection.
