## Front

Low serum phosphate with HIGH urine phosphate suggests what?

## Back

Renal overexcretion (hyperparathyroidism, tubular dysfunction)

## Extra

Low serum + low urine = poor intake or excess binding.

Source: PHMD 205C, Bone and Mineral Metabolism Labs.
