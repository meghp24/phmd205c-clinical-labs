## Front

Why can serum Mg look normal despite total body depletion?

## Back

Serum holds only about 1% of body Mg

## Extra

Mg wasting: PPIs, diuretics, cisplatin, calcineurin inhibitors. Replace Mg before expecting Ca or K to correct.

Source: PHMD 205C, Bone and Mineral Metabolism Labs.
