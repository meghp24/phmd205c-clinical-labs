## Front

Hypermagnesemia: Mg range where deep tendon reflexes drop and BP falls?

## Back

8.5 to 12 mg/dL

## Extra

6 to 8.5: bradycardia, flushing, drowsiness. > 12: flaccid paralysis, coma, asystole.

Source: PHMD 205C, Bone and Mineral Metabolism Labs.
