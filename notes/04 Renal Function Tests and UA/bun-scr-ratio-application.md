## Front

BUN 36 mg/dL, SCr 0.9 mg/dL, bland UA. What does the ratio suggest?

## Back

Ratio 40 (above 20): a prerenal cause such as dehydration

## Extra

Liver failure lowers BUN, so it would not explain a high ratio.

Source: PHMD 205C, Renal Function Tests and Urinalysis.
