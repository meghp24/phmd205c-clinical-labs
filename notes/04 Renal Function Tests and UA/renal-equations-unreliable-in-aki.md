## Front

Why are Cockcroft Gault and CKD-EPI unreliable during AKI?

## Back

Both assume a steady state SCr, so they overestimate function

## Extra

AKI: SCr up ≥ 0.3 mg/dL in 48 h or ≥ 1.5 × baseline.

Source: PHMD 205C, Renal Function Tests and Urinalysis.
