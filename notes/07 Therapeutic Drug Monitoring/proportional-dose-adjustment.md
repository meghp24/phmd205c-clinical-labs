## Text

Linear kinetics, same interval: new dose = current dose × ({{c1::target trough / measured trough}})

## Extra

Example: 1,000 mg q12h with trough 25 and target 17.5: 1,000 × 17.5/25 = 700, so 750 mg q12h.

Source: PHMD 205C, Therapeutic Drug Monitoring.
