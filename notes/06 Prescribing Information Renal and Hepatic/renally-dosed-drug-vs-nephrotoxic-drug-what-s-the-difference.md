## Front

Renally dosed drug vs nephrotoxic drug: what's the difference?

## Back

Renally dosed: accumulates when kidneys clear it poorly. Nephrotoxic: damages the kidney

## Extra

A renally dosed drug is not necessarily nephrotoxic.

Source: PHMD 205C, Application: The Prescribing Information, Renal and Hepatic.
